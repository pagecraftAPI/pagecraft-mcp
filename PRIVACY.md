# Pagecraft privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Pagecraft publishes data tools (Actors) that run on the Apify platform. This policy covers Pagecraft tools used directly on Apify, through the Apify MCP server, or through an AI assistant connector such as Muse.

## What data the tools receive
Only what you or your assistant sends in a request: for example UK company numbers or names, website addresses, domains or search keywords. Pagecraft tools do not ask for your name, contact details, payment details or account passwords.

## How it is processed
Each request runs as an Apify run in the Apify account that makes the call. Inputs and results are stored and processed by Apify under Apify's own privacy policy (https://apify.com/privacy-policy) and storage rules. Results come from public sources, such as the Companies House register or public websites.

## What Pagecraft does not do
Pagecraft does not sell data, does not use it for advertising, does not build profiles of users, does not infer sensitive characteristics, and does not use it to train AI models.

## Personal data in results
Some results contain public personal data, for example company officer names from Companies House. If you store or use them, you are responsible for doing so lawfully (for example under UK GDPR).

## Deletion and contact
To ask about or delete data related to a request, contact pagecraftapi@gmail.com. Data stored in your own Apify account can be deleted by you in Apify Console.
